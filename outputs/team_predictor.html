How it works: the model learned from ~5,400 past T20s which pre-match signals (recent form, career win rate, experience) predict the winner. On unseen later matches it is right about 67–70% of the time — well above a coin flip, but far from certain, because T20 is a genuinely high-variance format. So this is a probability, not a prediction of certainty. Team form is frozen at the end of the dataset (not live). The live tool uses robust team-strength features (head-to-head is excluded, as it's data-thin). Data: Cricsheet. Research tool, not betting advice.
